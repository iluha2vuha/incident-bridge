# Learning Objectives

The exercise uses no more than these six learning objectives. Facilitator language should remain generic: "Follow your organisation's approved reporting, verification, and incident-response procedures."

## 1. Recognise Connected Warning Signs

Behaviour taught: notice when a business concern, such as payroll urgency, and a technical signal, such as suspicious sign-in activity, may be connected.

Supported by rounds: 1, 2, 4.

Facilitator should look for: participants sharing role-specific facts before deciding, rather than treating HR and Helpdesk signals as separate issues.

## 2. Verify Identity Before Sensitive Action

Behaviour taught: use trusted verification paths before changing payroll details, resetting credentials, or acting on urgent requests.

Supported by rounds: 1, 2, 5.

Facilitator should look for: recognition that speed is useful only when identity and authority are checked through approved channels.

## 3. Preserve Useful Evidence

Behaviour taught: keep messages, headers, mailbox rules, logs, timelines, and user reports available for investigation.

Supported by rounds: 1, 2, 3, 5.

Facilitator should look for: participants resisting the urge to delete suspicious artefacts before they are recorded or escalated.

## 4. Escalate Appropriately

Behaviour taught: involve the right incident-response or security process when the situation exceeds routine HR or Helpdesk handling.

Supported by rounds: 1, 3, 4.

Facilitator should look for: participants naming escalation as a coordination step rather than a way to avoid ownership.

## 5. Coordinate Business And Technical Responses

Behaviour taught: combine HR context and Helpdesk evidence before acting, especially when each role has only part of the picture.

Supported by rounds: 2, 3, 4, 5.

Facilitator should look for: teams asking what the other role knows, agreeing on sequencing, and avoiding isolated decisions.

## 6. Balance Containment, Evidence, Continuity, And Trust

Behaviour taught: weigh competing outcomes instead of maximising only speed, technical containment, or comfort.

Supported by rounds: 2, 3, 4, 5.

Facilitator should look for: discussion of trade-offs, such as disabling access versus payroll continuity or broad warnings versus unnecessary alarm.
